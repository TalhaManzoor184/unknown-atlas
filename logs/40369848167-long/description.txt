Deep inside Yale University's Beinecke Library rests the Voynich manuscript—a centuries-old codex filled with unreadable script and bizarre botanical illustrations. Despite carbon-dating the vellum to the early 15th century and subjecting the text to advanced statistical modeling, codebreakers have never been able to translate it. Is it a lost human language, a complex cipher, or an elaborate historical hoax?

Discover the known history, scientific discoveries, and enduring mysteries of the world's most mysterious book.
