## Voiceover script

**[HOOK]** Imagine hearing a low, constant drone that no one else can hear.

**[CONTEXT]** In the early 1990s, studies in Taos, New Mexico, found that about two percent of the population heard a persistent frequency between 32 and 80 Hertz.

**[EVIDENCE]** Some researchers point to low-frequency tinnitus or spontaneous ear noises. Others suspect industrial machinery or even high-pressure gas pipelines.

**[TWIST]** Curiously, soundproofing a home often makes it worse by blocking out other background sounds.

**[CONCLUSION]** The Hum remains an unexplained mystery. Follow along to explore more strange phenomena.
