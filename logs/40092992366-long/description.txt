In February 1959, nine experienced hikers died in the northern Ural Mountains under bizarre circumstances. Walk through the evidence, the decades of wild theories, and how recent official reviews and a 2021 avalanche modelling study changed our understanding of the Dyatlov Pass incident.

Discover the truth behind the abandoned tent, the severe trauma, and what really happened on Dead Mountain.
