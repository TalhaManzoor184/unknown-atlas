## Voiceover script

**[HOOK]** In October 2017, astronomers spotted our very first confirmed interstellar visitor.

**[EVIDENCE]** As it swung past the Sun, it accelerated without the dust or gas coma of a normal comet.

**[TWIST]** Because of this unexplained push and its unusual flat shape, a Harvard scientist proposed a radical idea. Could it be an artificial solar sail?

**[CONCLUSION]** Radio telescopes found no signals. Mainstream consensus quickly settled on a natural origin, like a nitrogen-ice fragment.
