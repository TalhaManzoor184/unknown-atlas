## Voiceover script

**[HOOK]** In nineteen ninety-seven, deep in the South Pacific, NOAA hydrophones picked up an ultra-low frequency sound louder than any known creature on Earth.

**[CONTEXT]** The noise was so massive it was detected on sensors over five thousand kilometers apart. Naturally, rumors of a giant sea monster began to spread.

**[EVIDENCE]** Researchers noted the audio sped up sixteen times sounded remarkably like an animal vocalization.

**[TWIST]** But by twenty twelve, scientists solved the mystery. It wasn't alive at all.

**[CONCLUSION]** Data from breaking icebergs matched the acoustic profile. The Bloop was actually the sound of a massive icequake. Follow for more strange stories from the atlas.
