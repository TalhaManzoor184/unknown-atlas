## Voiceover script

**[HOOK]** For nearly 170 years, modern searchers failed to find the lost Franklin expedition.

**[INTRO]** In 1845, two British ships vanished into the Arctic ice with 129 men.
  _Pronunciation: Erebus = AIR-uh-bus_

**[CONTEXT]** Back in 1854, explorer John Rae listened to local Inuit hunters. They described trapped ships and starving men.

**[EVIDENCE]** Victorian society dismissed their accounts. Yet generation after generation, Inuit families kept the exact locations alive in memory.

**[TWIST]** When researchers finally listened in 2014, they discovered HMS Erebus right where Inuit oral history said it went down.
  _Pronunciation: Erebus = AIR-uh-bus_

**[CONCLUSION]** Generations of spoken memory solved what Victorian technology could not. Follow for more strange history.
